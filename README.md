# Duelmetrics

A Yu-Gi-Oh! deck analysis tool for exploring opening-hand probabilities and making informed decisions about deck ratios.

**[Visit Duelmetrics](https://duelmetrics.onrender.com/)**

## Overview

Duelmetrics helps players understand how their card choices affect the hands they draw. Assign cards roles based on how they function in your deck, then use probability results to evaluate consistency and identify potential adjustments.

The goal is to make deck testing more accessible and give players useful information to support their own deck-building decisions.

## Features

- Import a deck using a `.ydk` file.
- Assign roles such as Starter, Extender, Brick, Hand Trap, Board Breaker, and Tech.
- Assign multiple roles to cards that serve more than one purpose.
- Analyze opening-hand probabilities to evaluate deck consistency.
- Use the results to compare card ratios and guide further testing.

## Getting Started

1. Open [Duelmetrics](https://duelmetrics.onrender.com/).
2. Upload your deck as a `.ydk` file.
3. Assign roles to your cards according to your deck's strategy.
4. Run the analysis and review your opening-hand results.
5. Adjust your ratios and repeat the analysis to explore different builds.

## Understanding the Results

Card roles depend on the deck and the situations in which a card is useful. A card may be both a starter and an extender, so role assignments should reflect how you intend to use it.

Opening-hand probabilities provide one measure of consistency. They do not determine whether every hand is playable or account for every interaction, matchup, or sequencing decision. Use the results alongside practical testing.
